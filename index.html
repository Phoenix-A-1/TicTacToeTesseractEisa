Tic Tac Toe Tesseract

1. Game Structure & Objective

Tic Tac Toe Tesseract is a 4D nested variant played across a 9×9×9 board containing 729 cells in total:

  • 9 Macro G-grids (outermost green borders) labelled with Greek letters: α, β, γ, δ, ε, ζ, η, θ, ι.
  • Each Macro G-grid contains 9 Micro E-grids (middle blue borders) labelled with English letters: A, B, C, D, E, F, G, H, I.
  • Each Micro E-grid contains 9 individual cells numbered 1 to 9.
  • Winning the Tesseract: Win 3 cells in a line to claim a Micro E-grid. Claim 3 Micro E-grids in a line to claim a Macro G-grid. Claim 3 Macro G-grids in a line to win the entire Tesseract!

2. Movement & Game Methods

Your cell selection restricts where your opponent is forced to play next based on the chosen method:

Method 1 (sent to G):

Cell position i inside an E-grid routes the opponent to Macro G-grid i.

Method 2 (blocks of 3):

Turns alternate within the current G-grid for 3 consecutive moves, then cell i routes to Macro G-grid i.

Method 3 (finish each G):

Players stay within the current G-grid until it is won or drawn, after which a free move routes to the next target.
